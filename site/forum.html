Account and privacy

To post, reply or vote you need a GitHub account. GitHub, Inc. hosts the forum and handles your account and posts under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Statement. This website only links to the forum: it embeds nothing from GitHub and sets no cookies. How reports and moderation are handled is in the privacy section.

Rules in short

The full forum rules are in section 4 of Privacy, contact and forum rules; the Polish version is binding.

  • Stay on topic: FaTrainer, in English or Polish.
  • Nothing illegal, no hate, threats or harassment, and no harassing other players in the game either.
  • No other people's personal data.
  • No game files, cracks, pirated copies, malware, or other people's mods and code without permission.
  • No spam or advertising.

Reporting a post

Use Report content on the post in GitHub, or write to contact@fatum.cc with the link, why the post is illegal or breaks the rules, your name and e-mail address, and a statement that you report in good faith. You get an answer with the decision. Anyone whose post is removed is told why and can appeal by e-mail within six months.
