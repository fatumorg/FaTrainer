Docs

For FaTrainer 2.3, Dying Light 1 on Windows and Linux.

1. Getting started

The trainer is one file, xinput1_3.dll, next to DyingLightGame.exe. FaTrainer, a small launcher, puts it there, keeps it up to date and starts the game with it; you never copy files by hand.

  1. Download FaTrainer from the install section and open it. It finds the game in your Steam libraries, or you pick the folder yourself. Add shortcut puts it on your desktop and in the Start menu, or in your Linux app menu.
  2. On Linux, add WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command% to the game's launch options in Steam once, and run the game through Proton.
  3. Press Play, then press Insert or F8 in game.

The trainer only runs when the game was started with Play. Start Dying Light from Steam and you play the normal game: the file stays in the folder but does nothing. Remove from game deletes it and puts back any xinput1_3.dll that was there before; Play puts it back. From launcher 2.4, FaTrainer notices when Dying Light is already running: Restart with FaTrainer closes the game and starts it again with the trainer, and Close Dying Light only closes it. Launcher 2.5 adds Start Cheat Engine: tick it and FaTrainer downloads the official Cheat Engine installer in the background and checks it, and the trainer installs Cheat Engine the first time the game starts and opens it with the game, in the game's own Proton prefix on Linux, attached to Dying Light even after a restart. Cheat Engine closes when the game closes.

2. The menu

Insert opens and closes the menu, and F8 always works too; you can pick another key in Settings. While the menu is open the game does not get your mouse and keyboard, so clicking a switch never swings your weapon.

Drag the edges of the window to make it bigger; it remembers the size. The sidebar groups the pages into Cheats, Modes and Items. Refresh, at the bottom of the sidebar, looks for your character and inventories again after a loading screen, if a page says it cannot find them.

Switches take effect right away. Sliders start at Normal, the game's own value, and go up to Max. Everything you turn on is undone when you turn it off again.

3. Player and Combat

Player

Health and stamina bars you can drag to any value, Refill health and stamina, God mode, Infinite stamina, Take less damage, Infinite grappling hook, Infinite UV flashlight, Slow down UV flashlight, Instant lockpicking, No fall damage, Movement speed and Jump height.

Combat

One hit kill, Kill enemies (every zombie and human within 80 m, so quest objectives count them), Infinite ammo, No reload, Infinite consumables, Unbreakable weapons UV flare throw speed (from 2.5 b25, up to x5, only for the UV flare; from 2.5 b26 for both UV flare items) and Melee speed (from 2.5 b1, a beta build; from 2.5 b14 it speeds up your animations while you hold a melee weapon, from 2.5 b19 up to x5, from 2.5 b20 the game's swing timing runs at that speed too, so you really swing faster, from 2.5 b21 heavy and charged attacks recover at that speed too, and from 2.5 b22 only your swings speed up, not climbing, the grappling hook or picking things up). One hit kill works when you host the game. To edit a single weapon, press Edit next to it on the Backpack or Stash page. From 2.5 b15 the editor shows every stat as a big editable value with the game's value beside it, Undo, colored rarity buttons and extras that fit the item (no reload, infinite ammo, unbreakable, infinite UV, infinite grappling hook), and your edits come back every time you start the game. Item Dupe (from 2.5 b15) keeps what you drop in your backpack too (from 2.5 b17 as an exact copy, and only when you really drop it), and No reload now also changes crossbow magazines. From 2.5 b18 No reload keeps the weapon in your hands fully loaded, so there is no reload hint and R switches bolts right away; from 2.5 b21 it also holds when a Night Hunter is in the session. Always 1 bullet for Last Wish (from 2.5 b20) keeps one round in the Last Wish revolver, so every shot is its explosive last round.

4. Skills

Experience

XP gain multiplies the experience you earn in every skill tree, the Survivor and Night Hunter trees included. The game's XP popup shows the boosted amount.

Levels and skill points

Each tree has a tile with its level, an XP bar you can drag, and -10, -1, +1 point, +10 and Max. +1 point gives you exactly the XP for the next level, like playing would. A level you lower stays lowered when you load the game again (from 2.5 b23 also for Survivor). Free skill points shows what the game shows in its skill menu; - and + add or take away points without changing the level, until you close the game.

Skill tree

The skill tree shows every tree laid out like the game's own skill menu: Survivor, Agility, Power, Driver, Legend, and the Night Hunter. Point at a skill to read what it does and what it needs.

  • Click a skill to learn it. Skills it needs are learned too.
  • Click a learned skill to remove it. Skills that need it are removed too.
  • Legend skills have levels: click adds a level, right click takes one away.
  • Learn all and Remove all do the whole tree.

Learned skills work like skills you buy in the game and are saved with your game. The Night Hunter tree is hidden while you play a survivor in Be The Zombie.

Be The Zombie ranks

Your PvP rank as a survivor and as the Night Hunter, one title at a time. In a match, the side you are not playing is grayed out.

5. Night Hunter and PvP

Be The Zombie is the mode where one player is the Night Hunter and invades the game of up to four survivors. The game that hosts a match is always a survivor's game, which decides some things for everyone.

Night Hunter page

Hunter god mode, Infinite hunter energy, Take less damage, Less UV damage, No ability cooldowns, Infinite spits, Sure-hit ground pound and Long camouflage. Spit keys let you throw each spit with a key of your own.

PvP page

Sliders for how far your attacks reach. As the Night Hunter: Pounce, Ground pound, Tackle, Claws and Spit hit radius. As a survivor: Death from above range, pull and height, Dropkick range, angle and height (angle and height from 2.5 b1, a beta build), Other kicks and ground pound, and Melee reach (from 2.5 b8: every melee weapon reaches further, from 2.5 b24 it changes the reach the game uses for your swings). Each side's sliders only work while you play that side. From 2.5 b11 the death from above and dropkick sliders always work against players, and they never reach zombies further than a normal kick, so E keeps kicking in story mode.

  • Pounce only lengthens the range: you pounce the survivor you aim at. In Be The Zombie your attack becomes a pounce whenever a survivor counts as a pounce target.
  • Spit hit radius only works when you host. The Night Hunter invades, so in a normal match his spits are decided by the survivor's game.
  • Dodge spit steps you aside from a spit that would hit you, after a human reaction time.
  • Throwable recharge (from 2.5 b15) brings UV flares, injectors, grenades, molotovs, throwing stars and mines back faster in Be The Zombie; at Max they are back right away and a throw uses none of the charge.
  • One hit kill on the Night Hunter drops him to 1 health. In a real match his own game decides his health.

6. Configs

A config is a set of switches and sliders you can load in one click. There are four built-in ones, Survivor Legit and Rage and Night Hunter Legit and Rage, on the PvP page and in Settings. New config saves what is on now under a name; your own configs can be loaded, saved over, renamed and deleted. Built-in configs can be deleted too, and Restore built-in configs brings them back. Configs are files in the fatrainer_configs folder next to the game.

7. Visuals and Prison

Visuals

Player ESP draws every player through walls: box, role, health bar, distance, PvP rank, hunter rage and lines, with a range limit and separate colors for the Night Hunter and survivors. It keeps drawing while the menu is closed. You can also give the UV light and the Night Hunter's glowing veins your own color. Every color, the accent too, has a color picker, R, G and B fields from 0 to 255 and a hex field (from 2.5 b3, a beta build). HUD colors (from 2.5 b4, a beta build) tint the whole HUD or give each piece its own color; from 2.5 b9 every piece is separate (health bar, legend level bar, stamina, crosshair, weapon, radar, quest list, killfeed, subtitles, enemy health, the Night Hunter HUD and more, 35 in all), and Hide makes any piece invisible. Every color can also cycle through the rainbow at a speed you pick (from 2.5 b9). Crosshair (from 2.5 b9) draws your own crosshair (cross, dot, circle or cross and dot, with size, thickness, gap, opacity, outline and color) and can hide the game's. Survivor glow (from 2.5 b9) recolors how survivors glow in the Night Hunter's sense: normal, hit by UV Suppressor, marked by Horde Summoner and hit by Sense Suppressor. Texts (from 2.5 b5, a beta build; its own page from 2.5 b10) lets you search every text the game shows, in the language you see it, and replace it, like "Pounce interrupted by UV light!" or the killfeed; keep %s where the game puts a name. From 2.5 b10 it can also show the game's texts in another language (English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Czech, Dutch or Turkish) while the voices stay, and you choose for each group (spoken lines, items, skills, quests, tutorials, HUD, menus) whether it switches. The trainer reads those texts from the game's own files. From 2.5 b11 the Texts page has three separate settings instead: Game language (the game's own language switch, so menus, the Night Hunter info and everything else change natively, from the next start), Voices (the language the characters speak, from the next start) and Subtitles (right away). From 2.5 b22 every other text category (items, skills, quests, tutorials, HUD and messages, menus) can have its own language too, also right away. Be The Zombie callouts turn the killfeed into short lines like Pounced:, Failed Pounce:, Tackled: and Failed Tackle:, with optional colored labels, and you can click any text to edit it right where it is, color it and preview it. From 2.5 b14 the callouts use the killfeed's own colors and cover dropkicks, drop attacks, horde and UV Suppressor hits, toxic kills and more, and Preview posts a text into the game's real killfeed. From 2.5 b18 the callouts also cover every way to kill (sliced, shot, burned, blown up, run over and more), and their colors work in XP popups too. From 2.5 b20 callouts come in sets: two built-in sets (plain and colored) and your own, which you can create, rename, delete and edit line by line; the built-in sets can be edited too and restored with one click. With voices in another language than the game, the game starts in the voice language from 2.5 b18 and the trainer shows every text in your game language. Field of view (from 2.5 b11) scales the game's field of view live. Unlock every resolution (from 2.5 b26) lists every common resolution in the game's video settings, also sizes your monitor does not report, from the next start. From 2.5 b26 the nest counter drains again when HUD colors are on.

Visuals Version

Tick Visuals only in the FaTrainer launcher (2.3 or newer) and Play installs the Visuals Version instead of the full trainer: only the UV light, Night Hunter and survivor glow colors, HUD colors, the crosshair, the field of view, texts, the game, voice and subtitle languages, plus the menu settings. It has no cheats and no ESP, never connects to the internet and also runs when you start Dying Light from Steam. Untick Visuals only and press Play to get the full trainer back. It comes with 2.5 and the 2.5 beta builds from 2.5 b6.

Prison

For the Harran Prison run: pause the run timer and the reward room countdown, teleport to any split after Find sections, and record your route once to replay it, so every quest trigger along the way fires.

8. Items

Cash sets your money. Backpack, Stash, Materials and Tools list what you carry and store; change any stack count, or press Delete to remove an item (setting a count to 0 removes it too). Press Edit next to a weapon, crossbow, shield or other item to change its damage, durability, crits, reach, magazine, reload and rarity. Four upgrade slots take any upgrade and the mod any of the game's weapon mods, also ones the weapon would not take; the game keeps both in your save. Effects of a mod (Shock, Impact, Burning, Poison, Bleeding, Freezing, Stun, Explosive, Acid) can be turned off or set to any level the game has, for every weapon with that mod, and Upgrade slots sets how many slots the game's own upgrade screen offers for that weapon type. Give items finds any weapon, upgrade, consumable or blueprint by name and puts it into the inventory the game accepts it in.

9. Settings and keybinds

Controls
Remember cheats (turn your cheats back on at the next start) and, in the Fatum Version, Use older versions and Beta builds.
Keybinds
The menu key, a key to refill health and stamina (from 2.4) and one key per cheat, grouped by page. Press a cheat's key while the menu is closed to turn it on or off; a message at the bottom of the screen says which. From 2.5 b16 every slider can have a key too: one press sets it to Max, the next press puts back what you had. From 2.5 b20 there are keys for Kill all enemies nearby, Save position, Go to saved position and Player ESP on / off as well. A key belongs to one thing only, so picking a key takes it away from whatever had it. F8 always opens the menu and cannot be bound.
Appearance
Accent color, opacity, corner roundness, dimming, animations Full, Subtle or Off, the Harran skyline and glow.
Sidebar
Which pages show up and in what order.
Settings file
Everything is saved in fatrainer.ini next to the game. Reset to defaults starts over.

10. Updates and versions

New features first come out as builds of the next version, like 2.3 b1, 2.3 b2 and 2.3 b3. The last build of a version is called just by the version, like 2.3. Every version is also named after its most important change: 2.2 is the Skill Tree Update, 2.3 the Launcher Update. The changelog names them all.

  • A newer build of your version downloads itself when the game starts, is checked against its checksum and starts the next time you open the game. Until then your build keeps working.
  • With Latest chosen, Play installs the newest version first. When a newer version comes out while you play, the trainer stays off at the next start until you press Play in FaTrainer again. The game itself keeps working.
  • To play an older version, pick it from the version list in FaTrainer and press Play. Play turns on Use older versions so it keeps working; the notice still shows. Versions before 2.3 also run when you start the game from Steam.
  • When a newer FaTrainer is out, it updates itself as soon as you open it.

Beta builds

Beta builds are the version being worked on right now, open to everyone. They are never needed and may have bugs. Tick Beta builds in FaTrainer, or turn it on in Settings, and Play and the trainer also use the beta build whenever it is newer than the latest release. Turn it off to go back to the published releases only.

The Fatum Version only downloads files from GitHub and sends nothing else. A trainer started from Steam connects to nothing. When there is an announcement, such as a new version or a known problem, FaTrainer shows it once when it opens. The Nexus Version from Nexus Mods is 1.9, the trainer file alone without multiplayer features, and it is not updated any more. It never connects to the internet and does not update itself.

11. When something goes wrong

The menu does not open

Start the game with Play in FaTrainer, not from Steam, and press Insert or F8 in the game, not on the desktop. If FaTrainer says the game did not load it, close the game and press Play again. On Linux, the launch option WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command% must be set and the game must run through Proton.

A page says your character was not found

Load into your save, wait until the loading screen is gone and press Refresh.

The game closes or crashes

The trainer writes fatrainer.log next to DyingLightGame.exe. It records what the trainer did and, when the game crashes, where. Attach it when you report a problem or ask in the forum, together with Windows or Linux and your Proton version. From 2.5 b11 a window with the log and a Copy log button opens when the game crashes, or at the next start if the game closed too fast. Some antivirus programs stop games that load extra DLLs; try once with yours paused or with the game folder added as an exception.

Start from a clean slate

Press Reset to defaults in Settings, or delete fatrainer.ini while the game is closed. To remove the trainer, press Remove from game in FaTrainer.

Before you change your saves

Items, levels, skills and ranks are saved like any other progress. Keep a copy of your save folder before you try something big, and switch cheats off when you play with people who did not agree to them.
