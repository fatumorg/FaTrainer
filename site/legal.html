Privacy, contact and forum rules

In force from 11 October 2026. This is a translation; the Polish version is binding.

1. Who runs this site

The website at fatrainer.fatum.cc (the Website) is a private, non-commercial project of:

Name
Adam Pietrzak
E-mail
contact@fatum.cc

The Website and the FaTrainer trainers are free. Nothing is sold, there are no ads and no accounts, and the Website is not run as a business. Each trainer is open source under the MIT License, which also says it comes without warranty; its source is linked from its page. The Fatum Version of the installer and the trainers only downloads files from GitHub: the installer reads the list of trainer releases and downloads the trainer, and a trainer downloads a small version file when the game starts to check for updates, plus the newer trainer file when a newer build of its version is out; with Beta builds turned on, both also read the version file of the beta build. GitHub, Inc. handles these downloads under its own privacy statement and sees the IP address of your computer, as with any download. Nothing else is sent; the trainers write their settings and a log in the game folder. The Nexus Version of the installer and trainer, downloaded from Nexus Mods, makes no internet connections at all.

Game names are trademarks of their owners; Dying Light is a trademark of Techland S.A. FaTrainer is a fan project and is not made, supported or endorsed by any game publisher.

2. Privacy

This information is given under Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Controller

The controller of personal data is the person named in section 1, reachable at the e-mail address given there.

What data, and why

  1. Server logs. Each page view makes the server and Cloudflare record the IP address, date and time, the requested page, the referring page, the browser type and the response code. Purpose: delivering the page, security and preventing abuse. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Kept for 14 days, unless an entry is needed to investigate a specific security incident.
  2. E-mail. If you write to the controller, your e-mail address and message are used to reply. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest in answering (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Kept until the conversation is over.
  3. Forum reports and moderation. When you report content or appeal a decision (section 4), your name, e-mail address, the report and the reported content are used to handle it and to keep a record of the decision. Legal basis: the legal obligations of Articles 16, 17 and 20 DSA (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Kept until the case is closed and the six months for an appeal have passed, or longer if needed to establish or defend legal claims. Your posts and your GitHub account are processed by GitHub, Inc. as controller.

The Website uses no analytics, no advertising, no social media plugins and no fonts loaded from other servers. Providing data is voluntary, but a page cannot be shown without an IP address, and a message cannot be answered without an e-mail address. Data is not used for profiling or automated decision-making.

Recipients

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which routes traffic to the Website and protects it from attacks, as a processor under a data processing agreement.
  • The company that hosts the server, as a processor.

Links to GitHub lead to another controller (GitHub, Inc.), which processes data under its own privacy policy.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Cloudflare, Inc. may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in which Cloudflare, Inc. participates, and additionally on the Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict its processing and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To use them, write to the controller's e-mail address. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, or with the authority in your own EU country.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website stores no cookies or other data on your device (for example in localStorage), so it does not ask for consent.

The one exception can be the __cf_bm cookie, which Cloudflare sets for about 30 minutes when it needs to tell automated traffic from people. It is strictly necessary to secure the Website, so under Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024 it needs no consent. You can delete and block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Forum rules

These rules apply to the FaTrainer forum at GitHub Discussions (the Forum). They are the terms of service for the Forum under Article 14 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act, DSA) and the rules required by Article 8 of the Polish Act of 18 July 2002 on providing services by electronic means.

4.1 How the Forum works

  1. The Forum is free and run by the person named in section 1 (the Moderator). GitHub, Inc. hosts it. Anyone can read it; to post you need a GitHub account, which means accepting GitHub's Terms of Service and Privacy Statement and meeting GitHub's minimum age.
  2. The Website does not embed the Forum: it only links to it, so visiting the Website loads nothing from GitHub and sets no cookies.
  3. The Forum is for FaTrainer: questions, bug reports, ideas and help. Write in English or Polish.

4.2 What is not allowed

  1. Content that is illegal under Polish or EU law, including threats, incitement to violence, hate speech, defamation, child sexual abuse material and terrorist content.
  2. Personal data of other people without their consent, including their real names, addresses or photos.
  3. Infringing other people's rights: no game files, cracks or pirated copies, no other people's mods or code posted without permission, no keys or accounts for sale.
  4. Malware, links to malware or tools for attacking other people's computers or accounts.
  5. Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.
  6. Harassing or insulting other users, and organising the harassment of other players in Dying Light.

4.3 Reporting content

  1. Anyone can report content they consider illegal or against these rules by e-mail to contact@fatum.cc (the notice and action mechanism under Article 16 DSA), or with GitHub's own Report content option.
  2. A report should include: the link to the content, why you consider it illegal or against the rules, your name and e-mail address, and a statement that you believe in good faith that the report is accurate and complete. A report about child sexual abuse material may be anonymous.
  3. The Moderator confirms receipt, decides in a timely, careful, non-arbitrary and objective manner, and tells the reporter the decision and how to challenge it. No automated means are used to decide.

4.4 Moderation decisions and appeals

  1. Content against the law or these rules may be hidden, edited to remove the problem, locked, moved or deleted, and a user may be warned or blocked from the Forum. The measure is proportionate to the breach.
  2. The affected user is told, through GitHub or by e-mail if known, which measure was taken, the facts it is based on, which rule or law was broken, and how to appeal (statement of reasons under Article 17 DSA), unless the content is spam or the user cannot be reached.
  3. A user who frequently posts manifestly illegal content is first warned and then blocked for a reasonable time (Article 23 DSA).
  4. Appeals go by e-mail to contact@fatum.cc within six months of the decision. The Moderator reviews them personally and answers without undue delay. You may also use a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body (Article 21 DSA) or go to court.
  5. Information giving rise to a suspicion of a criminal offence that threatens someone's life or safety is reported to the police (Article 18 DSA).

4.5 Contact and responsibility

  1. The single point of contact for users and for authorities of the EU member states, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Articles 11 and 12 DSA) is contact@fatum.cc, in Polish or English.
  2. Posts are the opinions of their authors. The Moderator is not responsible for content they do not know about; once they learn that content is illegal, they act on it without delay.
  3. These rules may change when the Forum or the law changes. Changes are announced in the Forum at least 14 days before they apply, except where the law requires a faster change.
