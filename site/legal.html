Privacy, contact and forum rules

In force from 9 November 2026. This is a translation; the Polish version is binding.

1. Who runs this site

The website at fatrainer.fatum.cc (the Website) is a private, non-commercial project of:

Name
Adam Pietrzak
E-mail
contact@fatum.cc

The Website and the FaTrainer trainers are free. Nothing is sold, there are no ads, an account is only needed to write in the Forum (section 4), and the Website is not run as a business. Each trainer is open source under the MIT License, which also says it comes without warranty; its source is linked from its page. The Fatum Version of the FaTrainer launcher and the trainers only downloads files from GitHub: the launcher reads the list of trainer releases, the version files of the trainer and of itself and the current announcement and downloads the trainer and its own updates (with Visuals only ticked, the version file and the file of the Visuals Version instead of the full trainer), and a trainer started from the launcher downloads a small version file when the game starts to check for updates, plus the newer trainer file when a newer build of its version is out; with Beta builds turned on, both also read the version file of the beta build. A trainer started from Steam instead of the launcher connects to nothing. GitHub, Inc. handles these downloads under its own privacy statement and sees the IP address of your computer, as with any download. Nothing else is sent; the trainers write their settings and a log in the game folder. The Nexus Version, the trainer file alone downloaded from Nexus Mods, makes no internet connections at all, and neither does the Visuals Version, the trainer with only the looks that the launcher installs when Visuals only is ticked.

Game names are trademarks of their owners; Dying Light is a trademark of Techland S.A. FaTrainer is a fan project and is not made, supported or endorsed by any game publisher.

2. Privacy

This information is given under Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Controller

The controller of personal data is the person named in section 1, reachable at the e-mail address given there.

What data, and why

  1. Server logs. Each page view makes the server and Cloudflare record the IP address, date and time, the requested page, the referring page, the browser type and the response code. Purpose: delivering the page, security and preventing abuse. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Kept for 14 days, unless an entry is needed to investigate a specific security incident.
  2. E-mail. If you write to the controller, your e-mail address and message are used to reply. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest in answering (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Kept until the conversation is over.
  3. Forum account and posts. You sign in to the Forum with a Fatum Aura account (fatum.cc), run by the same controller. The Forum receives your account id, nickname, appearance settings and, depending on your visibility settings, your profile picture and bio. The Forum does not store your e-mail address: it reads it from your Fatum Aura account only when it sends a notification e-mail. The Forum keeps your threads, posts and their earlier versions, uploaded images, "Helpful" reactions, saved and watched threads, notifications, Forum settings and the date you joined. Purpose: running the Forum at your request. Legal basis: the contract for a service provided by electronic means (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Kept: posts stay until you delete them; after an account is deleted its posts stay under the name "Deleted account" and images of deleted posts are erased; images not attached to any post are erased after 24 hours; notifications, saved and watched threads and settings go with the account.
  4. Protecting the Forum from abuse. When posts, images or reports are sent, the server counts requests per IP address and per account to limit spam. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Kept: the counters expire after 10 minutes.
  5. Forum reports and moderation. When you report content or appeal a decision (section 4), the controller processes your account data or, for a report by e-mail, your name and e-mail address, together with the report, the reported content, the decision with its statement of reasons and the moderation log entry. Legal basis: the legal obligations of Articles 16, 17 and 20 DSA (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Kept until the case is closed and the six months for an appeal have passed, or longer if needed to establish or defend legal claims; the moderation log is kept for as long as the Forum runs.

The Website uses no analytics, no advertising, no social media plugins and no fonts loaded from other servers. Providing data is voluntary, but a page cannot be shown without an IP address, and a message cannot be answered without an e-mail address. Data is not used for profiling or automated decision-making.

Recipients

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which routes traffic to the Website and protects it from attacks, as a processor under a data processing agreement.
  • The company that hosts the server, as a processor.
  • The e-mail delivery provider, as a processor, when the Forum sends notifications.

Links to GitHub lead to another controller (GitHub, Inc.), which processes data under its own privacy policy.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Cloudflare, Inc. may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in which Cloudflare, Inc. participates, and additionally on the Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict its processing and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To use them, write to the controller's e-mail address. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, or with the authority in your own EU country.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website stores no cookies or other data on your device until you sign in to the Forum.

After you sign in, the Forum sets the __Host-ft_sid cookie, which keeps you signed in until you sign out, for at most 14 days; signing in through Fatum Aura sets that service's own cookies, described in its privacy policy. While you write a post, the editor keeps a draft in your browser's localStorage (keys starting with ft_draft:) so it survives a reload or signing in again; the draft is not sent anywhere and is removed once the post is published. These are strictly necessary for the service you ask for, so under Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024 they need no consent and the Website does not ask for it.

Cloudflare may also set the __cf_bm cookie, which Cloudflare sets for about 30 minutes when it needs to tell automated traffic from people. It is strictly necessary to secure the Website, so under Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law of 12 July 2024 it needs no consent. You can delete and block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Forum rules

These rules apply to the FaTrainer forum at fatrainer.fatum.cc/forum (the Forum). They are the terms of service for the Forum under Article 14 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act, DSA) and the rules required by Article 8 of the Polish Act of 18 July 2002 on providing services by electronic means.

4.1 How the Forum works

  1. The Forum is free and is run on their own server by the person named in section 1 (the Moderator), who may let chosen users moderate; their decisions are the Moderator's decisions.
  2. Anyone can read the Forum. To post, react, report or watch threads you need a Fatum Aura account with a confirmed e-mail address, created at fatum.cc under its terms, which also set the minimum age.
  3. The Forum is for FaTrainer: questions, bug reports, ideas and help. Write in English or Polish.
  4. New users (for their first 7 days or until they have 3 published posts) can start 3 threads and write 20 posts a day, and their posts with 3 or more links wait for a Moderator's approval. A suspended or banned account can read the Forum but cannot write.
  5. You can edit and delete your posts; the edit history is kept, and a deleted post leaves a note in the thread. Before deleting your account you can delete your posts; the rest stay visible under the name "Deleted account".

4.2 What is not allowed

  1. Content that is illegal under Polish or EU law, including threats, incitement to violence, hate speech, defamation, child sexual abuse material and terrorist content.
  2. Personal data of other people without their consent, including their real names, addresses or photos.
  3. Infringing other people's rights: no game files, cracks or pirated copies, no other people's mods or code posted without permission, no keys or accounts for sale.
  4. Malware, links to malware or tools for attacking other people's computers or accounts.
  5. Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.
  6. Harassing or insulting other users, and organising the harassment of other players in Dying Light.

4.3 Reporting content

  1. Anyone can report content they consider illegal or against these rules (the notice and action mechanism under Article 16 DSA): when signed in, with the "Report" button on a post or thread, or, also without an account, by e-mail to contact@fatum.cc.
  2. A report by e-mail should include: the link to the content, why you consider it illegal or against the rules, your name and e-mail address, and a statement that you believe in good faith that the report is accurate and complete. A report about child sexual abuse material may be anonymous.
  3. The Moderator decides in a timely, careful, non-arbitrary and objective manner and tells the reporter the decision by a Forum notification or by e-mail. No automated means are used to decide.

4.4 Moderation decisions and appeals

  1. Content against the law or these rules may be edited to remove the problem, deleted or moved, a thread may be locked, and a user may be suspended for a set time or banned. The measure is proportionate to the breach.
  2. The affected user gets a Forum notification and an e-mail stating the measure, the rule broken, the facts and the reasons, and how to appeal (statement of reasons under Article 17 DSA).
  3. A user who frequently posts manifestly illegal content is first warned and then suspended for a reasonable time (Article 23 DSA).
  4. You can appeal from the decision page the notification links to, or by e-mail to contact@fatum.cc, within six months of the decision. Someone other than the person who decided reviews it when the Forum has more than one Moderator, otherwise the person named in section 1; the answer comes without undue delay. You may also use a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body (Article 21 DSA) or go to court.
  5. Every moderation decision is recorded in a moderation log whose entries cannot be changed or deleted.
  6. Information giving rise to a suspicion of a criminal offence that threatens someone's life or safety is reported to the police (Article 18 DSA).

4.5 Contact and responsibility

  1. The single point of contact for users and for authorities of the EU member states, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Articles 11 and 12 DSA) is contact@fatum.cc, in Polish or English.
  2. Posts are the opinions of their authors. The Moderator is not responsible for content they do not know about; once they learn that content is illegal, they act on it without delay.
  3. These rules may change when the Forum or the law changes. Changes are announced in the Forum at least 14 days before they apply, except where the law requires a faster change.
